# On-Policy Morphing into Hybrid Attention Models

> 我们今天来聊一篇关于大型语言模型效率优化的研究，它要解决的核心问题是：在处理长文本时，像Transformer这样的大模型，虽然能力很强，但计算成本和内存消耗都非常巨大，尤其是随着文本长度增加，成本会呈平方级增长，这让它在实际应用中变得非常

**摘要 (EN):** Hybrid attention models combine full and linear attention to achieve a better quality--efficiency trade-off. Converting pretrained Transformers into such models requires identifying which layers should retain full attention under a prescribed budget. We identify two off-policy mismatches in FlashMorph's layer selection: (i) continuous branch interpolation and teacher-forced hidden states differ from discrete hybrid computation; and (ii) frozen model weights fail to account for parameter adaptation during subsequent recovery. We introduce FlashMorph2, an on-policy layer-selection method that (I) evaluates sampled discrete hybrid architectures end to end using their own hidden states, and (II) jointly optimizes layer-selection scores and model weights under the recovery objective to reflect post-adaptation performance. Experiments across Qwen3 model scales and linear-attention variants show that FlashMorph2 consistently outperforms existing layer-selection methods, improving average scores over FlashMorph by +2.42 points on RULER, +1.08 on LongBench, and +0.76 on recall-intensive tasks, while completing layer selection in only 0.72--3.57 GPU hours across 0.6B--8B models, demonstrating high efficiency and scalability.
