**摘要 (EN):** Computer-use agents (CUAs) are rapidly improving, yet their evaluation remains largely static. Existing benchmarks provide a strong foundation, but fixed difficulty ceilings, limited feature coverage, and coarse aggregate scores progressively weaken their discriminative and diagnostic value. We introduce CUA-SEE, a closed-loop evaluation environment that co-evolves tasks and executable verifiers from rollout evidence. It disentangles three objectives: benchmark evolution expands evaluation scope through cohort-calibrated difficulty and coverage growth; diagnostic evolution increases resolution through agent-conditional capability-boundary probes; and verifier evolution improves precision by translating changing requirements into state-grounded checkpoints with adversarial validation. Across multiple agents, applications, and rounds, CUA-SEE generates challenging, measurable tasks while preserving process-level evidence. Our results position adaptive evaluation as a scalable counterpart to self-evolving agents, continuously tracking what agents can do, where their boundaries lie, and how reliably progress is measured.
