# DecisionRank: On-Policy Distillation for Decision-Model Rerankers

> 文档重排序这个任务，说起来不复杂：给定一个用户查询，从检索系统返回的一百个候选文档里，把最相关的那些推到前面去。但要把这件事做得又快又好，一直是个难题。

**摘要 (EN):** Decision models target structured tasks by directly predicting choices or scores, avoiding autoregressive decoding latency. However, transferring a generative LLM's ranking knowledge to a decision model remains challenging: the teacher expresses preferences through a ranked list of document ID sequences, while the student determines the ranking using scores from the first-token position. To bridge this gap, we introduce DecisionRank, a decision model trained through item-space on-policy distillation (OPD). Item-space OPD constructs the student's rollout by sorting its document scores into a ranked sequence. A generative teacher takes the resulting ranking list as inputs and provides conditional feedback to directly supervise those scores. Combined with offline ranking supervision, DecisionRank achieves state-of-the-art performance on both general and reasoning-intensive retrieval benchmarks such as TREC DL, BEIR, and BRIGHT. Across four benchmarks, item-space OPD consistently outperforms alternative training objectives. Even at 1.7B parameters, DecisionRank surpasses its 27B generative teacher on TREC DL19--23. At 8B, it outperforms its generative listwise counterpart while running 24.5times faster on TREC DL and 16.7times faster on BRIGHT.
