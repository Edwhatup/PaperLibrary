# Antom 3-in-1 Transformer: A Payment Foundation Model

> 支付风险检测这件事，表面上看是一道分类题：这笔交易是不是欺诈？但真正做过的人都知道，它的难点不在于算法选择，而在于数据的异构性。同一个平台上，跨境电商、数字娱乐、旅行航空、AI服务，每一类商户的用户行为模式都不一样；欺诈者还会不断调整手法，

**摘要 (EN):** We report the development of Antom 3-in-1 Transformer, a payment foundation model that integrates sequence, tabular, and graph representations for payment risk detection. The model contains approximately 7B parameters, with approximately 1.7B activated per task route. Antom 3-in-1 Transformer is pretrained to learn reusable behavioral and relational representations, primarily on Ant International's internal payment data. In a cross-business transfer experiment, pretraining improves the target business metric by 34.5% over the same architecture trained from scratch. With only 25% of the training labels, the pretrained model outperforms the full-label scratch baseline in Average Precision (AP) and Recall@0.5% on different merchant domains. In a four-month online experiment on Ant International's billion-scale payment platform, the model reduced the overall loss rate by 37.88% relative to XGB-Online.
