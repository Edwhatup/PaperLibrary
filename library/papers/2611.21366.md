# ALIVE: Making Streaming Video Models Interactive

> 现有的流式视频大模型能持续看视频，但它们基本上是被动的——你问它一个问题，它回答一个问题，然后就等着。它不会主动监视你交代的任务，不会在你改变指令时调整行为，更不会在信息不足的时候主动向你要更多线索。这篇来自清华、北大、字节跳动等机构的论文

**摘要 (EN):** Streaming video LLMs can continuously observe the world, yet often handle user requests in isolation. We introduce ALIVE, an interactive streaming video model supporting proactive monitoring, streaming task coordination, and feedback-seeking interaction. We construct ALIVE-SFT-288K and ALIVE-RL-3K for two-stage training. Contrastive supervised fine-tuning teaches when and how to respond, while reinforcement learning with Behavior-Aligned Credit Assignment (BACA) combines trajectory-level and local advantages to refine decisions, responses, and silence across multi-turn interactions. We also introduce FSI-Bench to evaluate feedback seeking and subsequent task completion. ALIVE achieves the highest overall scores among the compared models on four public benchmarks and FSI-Bench, demonstrating strong streaming understanding and interactive capabilities.
