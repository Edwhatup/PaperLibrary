# ProxyGenBench: Benchmarking Proxy-Guided Video Generation

> 视频生成领域有一个长期悬而未决的问题：我们已经能让模型生成画面逼真的视频，但要精确控制一个场景怎么展开，依然非常困难。角色在哪里、怎么移动、镜头怎么切、事件按什么顺序发生——这些空间和时间层面的结构性要求，单靠文字描述很难说清楚。这篇来自腾

**摘要 (EN):** Recent video generators produce realistic videos, yet controlling how scenes unfold remains challenging. Structural proxies, such as animated 3D blockouts, guide generation by specifying scene layout, motion, camera behavior, and event order. However, these coarse inputs leave details unspecified and may contain artifacts that should not be reproduced. Successful proxy-guided generation must therefore Preserve intended structure, Complete missing content and correct proxy artifacts, and Bind text, image, and audio conditions to the right entities and events. We introduce ProxyGenBench to study these interacting requirements, with 312 evaluation cases from 60 scenes across three scene tasks and controlled variations in proxy fidelity, text detail, and multimodal inputs. We evaluate five video generators using verified, case-specific checklists. Experiments show that proxies improve spatiotemporal control, but greater proxy detail can coincide with poorer completion, while strong structural adherence does not ensure correct binding.
