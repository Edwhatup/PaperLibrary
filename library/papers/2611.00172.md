# From Fragmentation to Fusion: A Survey and Roadmap for Omnimodal Embeddings

> 嵌入模型研究长期以来被一道无形的墙分割成互不相通的孤岛——文本、图像、音频、图结构、时间序列、表格，各自为政，各有各的社区、各有各的基准测试、各有各的方法论。这篇论文要解决的核心问题就是：这种碎片化状态是否合理？它是否正在阻碍整个人工智能领

**摘要 (EN):** Embedding models underpin virtually every modern AI system, from search engines to recommendation systems. Despite shared mathematical foundations, research on embedding models remains fragmented by modality: the communities develop largely in isolation, seldom exchanging insights. We argue that this fragmentation obscures a deeper convergence. Driven by contrastive learning, Transformer architectures, and a granularity shift, embedding research across modalities is converging toward a common set of principles, even as modality-specific challenges remain underexplored. To reason across this convergence without conflating it, we propose a framework of three progressively harder capabilities: coverage, comparability, and compositionality. Three paths toward unification satisfy these in turn: transformation broadens coverage, alignment enables comparability, and joint embedding, the terminus we advocate, aims at compositionality. This position paper traces the resulting evolution, providing the first unified, cross-cutting treatment of omnimodal embedding research.
