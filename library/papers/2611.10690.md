# JoVox: Joint Hierarchical Flow Matching for Lightweight Voxel Generation

> 在三维计算机视觉领域，生成高质量的体素模型，同时保持其轻量级的特性，一直是一个悬而未决的难题。体素，你可以把它想象成三维空间里的像素点，它们记录了一个三维形状在空间中的占据情况。这种表示方法非常轻量，而且应用广泛，比如可以把体素转换成更精细

**摘要 (EN):** Generating high-fidelity voxels while keeping their lightweight nature remains an open problem in computer vision. Current generators are either high-fidelity but computationally heavy, or lightweight but of lower fidelity. Hierarchical methods keep the lightweight nature of voxels. However, their coarse levels, which decide where all finer cells can appear, are generated with the least information about the shape and are never revisited, so they are often plausible but wrong. Our key insight is to let finer cells take part in deciding coarse cells. To this end, we propose JoVox, a discrete flow matching framework that learns the joint distribution of all levels of the voxel hierarchy, instead of a separate distribution for each level, so that coarse cells can be predicted with finer cells already generated nearby. Within this framework, we further introduce a two-level prediction, which outputs the cells two levels finer at once instead of one level finer, halving the number of levels that the network processes and yielding voxels of higher fidelity. We also introduce an unmasking strategy that revisits early decisions once finer cells are generated and corrects inconsistencies in masked diffusion models. Our framework outperforms the baselines on both unconditional and image-conditioned voxel generation. Compared with the current state-of-the-art TRELLIS.2, we achieve 8x higher training throughput, 1/15 of the FLOPs in a forward pass, train on 4 vs. 32 GPUs, and obtain higher quality with an 11% lower Chamfer distance.
